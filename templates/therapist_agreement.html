SELF-EMPLOYED THERAPIST AGREEMENT

Business: Echo Kushu

Trading Name: Kushu Thai massage

Business Address: 48 GEORGE STREET,BRIGHTON, BN2 1RJ

This Agreement sets out the working arrangements, responsibilities and standards expected of therapists providing services to the Business.

1. SELF-EMPLOYED STATUS

Unless the therapist has specifically entered into a separate employment agreement with the Business, the therapist provides their services on a self-employed basis.

The therapist is responsible for their own tax affairs, National Insurance contributions and any other liabilities arising from their self-employed work.

The therapist is responsible for managing their working availability and informing the Business of the hours and shifts they are available to work.

Nothing in this Agreement is intended to remove or restrict any statutory employment rights that may apply according to the actual working relationship between the parties.

2. SHIFTS AND CANCELLATIONS

Therapists must provide the Business with at least 24 hours' notice if they are unable to attend an agreed shift.

If a therapist cancels an agreed shift with less than 24 hours' notice and a customer is already booked for treatment during that shift, a £20 charge may apply, subject to applicable law and the circumstances of the cancellation.

The therapist should notify the Business as soon as reasonably possible in an emergency, illness or other exceptional circumstance.

3. THERAPIST PAYMENTS

Payment for completed treatments will normally be made between 2 and 14 days following the treatment, subject to the Business receiving the required treatment and payment records.

The therapist is responsible for checking the treatments they have completed and ensuring that amounts owed to them are accurately recorded.

4. DAILY TREATMENT AND PAYMENT RECORDS

At the end of each working day, the therapist must provide an accurate record of treatments completed and amounts they believe are owed to them.

Amounts that have not been properly recorded may be treated as unverified and subject to investigation before payment.

The Business will not deliberately withhold payment for work that can be independently established as having been completed.

5. TIPS

100% of tips given directly to an individual therapist belong to that therapist, subject to applicable UK tipping legislation and any agreed arrangements for tips intended to be shared between staff.

The Business will not retain tips belonging to individual therapists.

6. BREAKAGES AND DAMAGE

All damage or breakages to Business property, equipment or stock must be reported immediately, regardless of the value of the item.

Where damage has been caused deliberately, recklessly or through negligence, the therapist may be required, where legally permitted, to:

* pay the reasonable cost of repair;

* pay the reasonable replacement cost; or

* provide an equivalent replacement agreed with the Business.

Normal wear and tear, equipment failure and accidental damage not caused by negligence will be considered separately.

7. MOBILE PHONES

Personal mobile phones must not be used or kept in therapy rooms while treatments are taking place, except where required for an emergency or expressly authorised by the Business.

Phones should be kept securely:

* inside the therapist's bag in the designated staff area; or

* inside a staff locker where available.

This rule exists to protect customer privacy, confidentiality and the professional environment of the Business.

8. CUSTOMER CONFIDENTIALITY

Customer information is confidential.

Therapists must not copy, photograph, retain, disclose, transfer or use customer information for personal purposes or for another business without lawful authority.

This includes, where applicable:

* names;

* telephone numbers;

* email addresses;

* home addresses;

* booking information;

* treatment information;

* photographs;

* payment information; and

* any other personally identifiable information.

9. EXCHANGE OF CUSTOMER DETAILS

Therapists must not privately exchange customer contact details for the purpose of taking customers away from the Business or arranging unauthorised private appointments.

If customer details are exchanged for a legitimate reason, or if a customer voluntarily provides their details in circumstances relevant to safeguarding, complaints or Business operations, the therapist must report the matter to management as soon as reasonably possible.

Personal information must only be collected, stored and shared where there is a legitimate and lawful reason to do so.

10. OTHER BUSINESSES AND PRIVATE PRACTICES

Therapists must not actively promote, advertise or solicit customers for another massage, therapy or competing business while dealing with customers of the Business.

Therapists must not use confidential Business information or customer information to obtain private work.

Nothing in this clause is intended to prevent a genuinely self-employed therapist from carrying on an independent business where doing so does not misuse confidential information, customer information or other legitimate Business interests.

11. IDENTIFICATION AND CONTACT INFORMATION

The Business may require therapists to provide reasonable identification and contact information necessary for legal, insurance, right-to-work, safeguarding and administrative purposes.

This may include:

* full legal name;

* current residential address;

* telephone number;

* email address;

* appropriate identification;

* right-to-work documentation where legally required; and

* emergency contact information where voluntarily provided.

Copies of passports or other identity documents will only be requested, retained and processed where the Business has a lawful reason to do so.

Where a therapist has a genuine safeguarding concern regarding disclosure of their residential address or other personal information, they should raise this confidentially with management so that an appropriate alternative arrangement can be considered.

12. DATA PROTECTION

Both the Business and therapist must respect customer confidentiality and comply with applicable UK data-protection requirements.

Customer information must only be accessed for legitimate Business purposes and must be kept secure.

Suspected loss, theft or unauthorised disclosure of customer information must be reported to the Business immediately.

13. PROFESSIONAL CONDUCT

Therapists are expected to:

* behave professionally toward customers and other staff;

* maintain appropriate hygiene and presentation;

* respect customer privacy and dignity;

* follow reasonable health, safety and safeguarding procedures;

* maintain confidentiality;

* accurately record treatments performed; and

* immediately report serious incidents, complaints or safeguarding concerns.

14. SERIOUS BREACHES

Serious breaches of confidentiality, misuse of customer information, theft, deliberate damage, serious misconduct or serious safeguarding violations may result in the Business terminating the working relationship, subject to applicable contractual and legal requirements.

15. CHANGES TO THIS AGREEMENT

Any material changes to this Agreement should be communicated to the therapist and agreed where required.
